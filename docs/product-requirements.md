# Product requirements

Benchline Stats is a responsive rink-hockey application for an observer managing players, teams and live matches on a personal device. The prototype prioritizes reliable local capture and synchronization. The responsive recorder and completed-match analysis are implemented for review; broader analytics remain deferred.

## Current prototype scope

- Players: create, edit names, logically delete, search, list and view details, including locally available team memberships, jersey numbers and roles. Duplicate names are valid.
- Teams: create, logically delete, edit name/category/season, view details, assign multiple existing players or create missing Players from the assignment dialog, and edit their optional jersey numbers and field-player/goalkeeper roles. Creating a Player saves it immediately; cancelling assignment keeps that Player. Removing membership keeps the reusable Player and existing Match snapshots.
- Team forms offer Senior, U-19, U-17, U-15, U-13 and Veteran as category presets, while accepting custom category text. A year picker stores seasons as 2026/27; existing nonstandard season text stays editable. Both fields remain optional. New Teams default Season to the device's current starting year, such as 2026/27; it remains editable and clearable. Editing preserves saved values.
- Matches: choose a team, opponent and call-up; optionally specify date and venue; configure two equal halves during creation; save an incomplete call-up; edit details/call-up before starting.
- Start with exactly four field players and one goalkeeper, using the saved duration. Category defaults per half are 25 minutes for Senior/U-19/Veteran, 20 for U-17/U-15 and 18 for U-13. Duration remains editable during creation and pre-start detail editing; missing/custom categories require a choice. Existing records remain readable. The recorder can set attack direction for the current live period; cup-specific extra periods remain deferred.
- Live tracking: recovery including intercepted shots, loss, open-play/penalty/direct-free-hit shots, goalkeeper saves requiring simple/difficult classification for new recordings, own/opponent timeouts, player-attributed foul, yellow/blue/red card, paired substitution, clock-linked powerplay expiry/correction, fifth-player entry, opponent goal and score correction.
- Countdown clock with start/pause, manual adjustment and explicit period/match completion.
- Timeline and durable Undo of the latest eligible locally recorded event. Finishing a match replaces the live URL with its read-only summary. Old live/Start links resolve to the current lifecycle screen. The summary includes the final result, period-filtered team statistics, linked event map/timeline and individual player statistics including recorded playing time, shot outcomes and types, goalkeeper saves and attributed conceded goals, recoveries/losses, fouls and cards.
- Live and completed timelines use period/game-time order, with recording-order ties and explicitly untimed events last within each period. New events validate both preceding and later chronological history before saving. Consistent late timestamps support playing-time calculation; contradictory or incomplete timing remains unavailable. Saved event order, timestamps and attribution are never silently rewritten. A dedicated missing-event flow remains awaiting UI review.
- Completed analysis includes a paired-substitutions-only filter and one five-lane Team timeline spanning the selected periods, with a shared time scale, scrollbar and explicit period dividers. Incoming players continue the outgoing player's lane; these are not tactical positions. Player pills contain individually selectable event icons, without substitution markers, timestamp connector lines or free-space time tracking. Event hover/focus previews and selection link the timeline, rink and Game events list. Nearby icons use small local spacing adjustments in chronological order, with separate pointer targets and a minimum inset inside each pill. Names stay behind the markers without reserving time-axis space. Exact recorded times remain in tooltips and event rows; no stored timestamp is moved. Recorded stints stop at the first chronology conflict; the remaining region is explicitly uncertain. Missing period ends never become planned played time. Filters and selection never change saved data.
- English and European Portuguese throughout, including offline use.

The compact completed header emphasizes the date with a calendar icon, shows the linked locally available Team category and a compact venue indicator, and omits the completed-status pill, duration and large separator gap. Category is current Team metadata rather than a historical Match snapshot; omit it when unavailable and never infer it from duration. Away matches display the opponent first, with both final and half scores ordered consistently. Period selection is compact on desktop and retains touch-sized targets on phones. Team metrics share one restrained glass card with aligned primary values. Goals and conversion sit beside the shot count; on-target share sits beside its count. Goalkeeper cards retain the same surface treatment. The completed page uses reduced top padding and omits the redundant read-only caption. A compact accent player filter sits beside Match analysis. Heading sizes distinguish main statistics sections, analysis and its Team timeline/Game events panels. Timeline markers have transparent backgrounds and filled foul whistles. The rink uses a defence-to-attack gradient on its lines only, a compact positioned-event fraction and an information tooltip for normalized direction. The rink and its legend share the left analysis column on every layout; the event list stretches alongside the full group on desktop. Mint dividers distinguish periods in the list and team timeline, whose period labels remain visible during horizontal scrolling. All-events analysis includes compact Shots, Recoveries and Losses breakdowns with thicker bars and distinct subtype colours, without intervening separators; selecting a category retains its detailed breakdown. Event-row hover backgrounds have square corners. Timeout totals use one quiet compact row below analysis, respond to the selected period and remain independent of analysis player/category filters. Player-table rows are compact with explicit horizontal gutters, including the team total.

Completed-match mobile browsing uses compact selects for the global period and analysis-only event category, while desktop retains segments and category buttons. Home/Away sits beside the date. Mobile Game events renders the complete filtered history in a scrollable area about four rows tall, with native scroll chaining to the page at list boundaries and an explicit fullscreen action. Gestures starting outside the list scroll the page directly. The full list shares row rendering, keeps the selected event and restores focus/page position on close. Mobile player statistics use compact expandable items, sorting and team totals. The summary card has an outer inset on phones. Secondary browsing buttons use the existing neutral outlined style. Player and team-total details and their mint-capsule chevrons animate together for 200ms, with reduced-motion preferences respected. Reliable playing time includes a small min unit; unavailable time has no unit. Expansion reveals shot outcomes, fouls and coloured card counts; a separate View player events action applies the analysis filter. Desktop tables and all statistics calculations remain unchanged.

The canonical entity name is Match. Event attribution uses its MatchPlayer snapshot. See [Domain model](domain-model.md) for exact rules and [Recorder UX](recorder-ux.md) for entry behavior.

## Shot entry

Set piece is available from Game event and the non-rink Shot flow without an extra mandatory tap for ordinary shots. Penalty and Direct free hit each remain one shot with a fixed restart-spot position. Rink-first entry retains the observer's selected point and hides Set piece. Import and sharing/export remain separate backlog work.

## Entity removal

Player and Team deletion removes them from active lists and future selection while retaining their identities and existing Match history. Their current memberships are removed. Match deletion removes its call-up, events and local Undo history, retaining its Team and Players. Live Matches use an explicit discard confirmation; they are never silently completed. Membership removal only detaches a Player from one Team.

All removals require confirmation, expected revisions, atomic local writes and durable synchronization markers. Removed IDs cannot be reused or restored by stale uploads, acknowledgements or downloads. Pending commands remain visible if blocked; deletion does not silently discard them. Historical snapshots retain their original names, numbers, roles and statistics. This is operational removal, not erasure of retained historical identities or delivery receipts. Restore and permanent purging remain deferred.

## Offline and accounts

Closed-beta accounts are created by administrators without public signup or email workflows. The online-only Users page supports account creation, display-name editing, password replacement, disable/enable and explicit permanent deletion of disabled normal users. Creation uses a dedicated page. New/replacement passwords require 12–128 characters, cannot be blank-only and require matching confirmation. Admin accounts are protected; usernames and roles cannot be edited. Disabling retains server hockey data. Permanent deletion removes the account and its server data, while remote local data remains outside the administrator's control.

Require one successful online sign-in to establish the owner of the local dataset. After setup, all current hockey workflows use IndexedDB online and offline. Session expiry pauses synchronization without blocking local use. Guest functionality is deferred.

A match can be created offline using locally available team/player data, including prerequisites created offline. No per-match server preparation or server-issued ID is required. Data never downloaded or created on the device is unavailable there.

Save records and their outbox operations atomically before reporting success. Distinguish saved on this device, waiting, synced and blocked states. A Match's delivery status includes its setup, controls, events and Undo operations. Never silently discard failed uploads or overwrite pending work with downloads.

The fixed `/app/.../view?id=…` routes and Match Start/live routes cache generic pages; the browser selects records from IndexedDB. Unknown IDs show unavailable. Legacy Player/Team dynamic detail documents remain online-compatible.

Explicit sign-out requires confirmation and a warning for unsynced work, then deletes the entire application IndexedDB database, including unsynced records, after successful server sign-out. Generic installed application files remain. Cleanup failure offers retry. This personal-device policy does not encrypt local data or check fresh revocation while offline. See [Offline and sync](offline-sync.md) and [Account authentication](account-auth.md).

## Recording requirements

- Player-first, action-first and optional rink-first entry produce the same canonical event through one service.
- Capture current period, wall-clock capture time and any known game time at the first tap. Later choices and retries retain them.
- Keep unfinished drafts only while the modal is visible. Cancel writes nothing; reload discards unfinished selection.
- Every event includes its period. Substitution, fifth-player entry, non-yellow card and powerplay completion require game time. Other events may omit it.
- New recoveries/losses require a defensive/attacking half; entry without coordinates requests an explicit choice; legacy absent location remains unknown. New score corrections affect the opponent only; our goals remain tied to shot history.
- Optional observed coordinates contain both normalized team-relative x/y values. New penalties/direct free hits use fixed team-relative restart-spot coordinates. Rink-first entry follows the observer view and captures time/direction at first valid contact. Desktop shows the rink shortcut. Spatial event dialogs include optional position selection, with direct hold/drag precision and magnification. Recovery/loss half follows the point, with explicit choice on the halfway line. Ordinary events without rink selection have no coordinates; set pieces carry their fixed restart spot.
- Hide incompatible actions and players. Recheck the same rules inside local and server transactions.
- Keep four or five players on the field and at most one goalkeeper. Outstanding powerplay limits the team to four. Ordinary substitutions exchange two players atomically.
- Yellow has no lineup/powerplay effect. Blue/red affect team capacity even for bench recipients. The third blue triggers permanent red-card exclusion. Team-penalty timers use two minutes for blue and four for direct red, follow playing time and run sequentially; remaining time is correctable. Temporary individual suspension restrictions remain deferred.
- Derive score, lineup, card accumulation and powerplay from non-deleted history. Do not keep a second authoritative mutable copy.

## Acceptance baseline

The implementation should support these scenarios in a production build:

1. Create players, a team and a Match offline after initial sign-in. Reload without losing committed data.
2. Assign the same Player to different teams with independent numbers/roles. Match snapshots preserve historical details.
3. Capture an eligible event through either entry order. Missing required details block saving; optional position never blocks it.
4. Capture known time automatically while running or paused. Manual time permits required timed actions without a running clock.
5. Apply an active or bench card without an invalid lineup. Queue further penalties at four, expire them by playing time or correct them explicitly, then prompt for a fifth player without an outgoing player.
6. Undo the latest eligible event, including its score, lineup, powerplay and third-blue consequences.
7. Reconnect and upload once despite lost acknowledgements or duplicate delivery. Preserve newer local work through acknowledgements and downloads.
8. Pause sync on expiry or account mismatch; retain local work. Sign-out cleanup must not be reversed by late responses.
9. Switch language without losing draft, clock or pending work. Support narrow screens, enlarged text and keyboard/touch access.
10. End periods explicitly and complete the Match. Reopen it as a read-only summary.

## Deferred scope

The next recorder UI proposal uses one Add missing event action, choosing the period and event time before the ordinary event choices. It must preserve the main clock and validate the entire resulting history. Editing saved event time/player attribution and deleting individual events are agreed follow-up scope, pending UI and protocol review. Team timeline pill colours remain provisional. App-wide colour adoption in recorder choices/dialogs remains a high-priority backlog item. Completed analysis and recorder event lists share one icon mapping. Completed shot icons use mint for goals, blue for on-target, yellow for blocked and muted for off-target, independently of attempt type or filters. Desktop analysis uses a bounded viewport workspace for timeline, rink and list; short/narrow screens retain normal flow. Event details open on selection instead of occupying a permanent panel, and per-event position/delivery controls are omitted. Footer synchronization feedback remains available.

Cross-match analytics, heatmaps, arbitrary historical editing/deletion, redo, reopening completed matches, official foul rules, individual temporary suspensions, conflict resolution, simultaneous multi-device recording, backup/export, guest accounts, self-service password changes and admin-role management remain in the [Backlog](backlog.md). The approved completed-match summary uses recorded event positions without inferring missing locations or opponent action statistics.

The [Development plan](development-plan.md) distinguishes implemented features, verification evidence and outstanding release checks. Deferred goals are not current acceptance claims.
